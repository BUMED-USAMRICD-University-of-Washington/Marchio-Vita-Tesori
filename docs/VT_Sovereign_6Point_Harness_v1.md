Mechanical Rigging & Safety Specifications

1\. 6-Point Biomechanical Load Distribution

-   **The Architecture:** The harness splits deceleration or fall-arrest forces symmetrically across 6 fixed structural coordinates: dual over-the-shoulder straps, dual horizontal torso waist belts, and dual inner-thigh leg loops.
-   **The Geometry:** The leg and pelvic straps utilize a wide, non-squeezing crescent envelope lined with **340 GSM organic bamboo mechanical stretch interlock padding**. This naturally cradles the femoral arteries and lower genitalia under load, protecting the user from harness-hang syndrome without shifting.

2\. Dorsal Rigging Plate & Modular Back Attachment

-   **The Interface:** A solid, forged marine brass triangle dorsal plate is anchored directly between the shoulder blades. This acts as the universal back attachment point.
-   **The Hook Compatibility:** The geometry of this dorsal ring features a heavy-duty, reinforced internal clearance channel engineered to accept a standard **modular parachute deployment bag riser, heavy utility cargo hooks, or locking tactical carabiners** without binding or metallic shearing.

3\. Silver-Infused Antistatic Thread Rows

-   **The Protection:** High-altitude parachute deployment or fast-rope friction generates immense static electricity along heavy web straps. This harness features multi-row structural lockstitching utilizing our **99.9% pure silver-infused organic hemp thread**. This conductive mesh network creates a continuous grounding loop that bleeds static charges straight through the brass hardware components, shielding nested biosecurity arrays or electronics from static shock.
