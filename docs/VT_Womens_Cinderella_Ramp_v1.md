Mechanical & Material Engineering for the See-Through Aluminum Heel

1\. The ALON / Optical Crystal Monoblock Chassis

-   **The Material:** **Aluminum Oxynitride (ALON)** is an advanced crystalline ceramic that is optically transparent but possesses a hardness and structural strength roughly four times greater than standard silica glass or Pyrex. It cannot warp, permanently scratches less than steel, and handles high physical impact without fracturing.
-   **The Sizing Envelope:** To scale flawlessly across all sizes without pinching, the entire front vamp features a wide-set, round-oval toe box contour. The interior is lined with a precision-molded layer of clear, soft, organic non-toxic plant resin gel to cushion the ball of the foot directly against the solid ALON plate.

2\. The Unbroken True-Ramp Incline

-   **The Geometry:** The interior bed of the crystal block drops in a single, uninterrupted straight plane from the top of the ankle entry straight down to the forefoot joint. There is no flat heel seat.
-   **The Physics:** This unbroken slant uses gravitational forces to permanently slide the center of gravity forward, locking **100% of her weight strictly onto the metatarsal pads and toes**. Because the heel bone cannot rest flat, it acts solely as a vertical alignment point, eliminating the risk of bone bruising against the rigid crystal wall.

3\. The CIST Leather Security Anchor System

-   **The Problem:** Because solid crystal or ALON has absolute zero flex or give, a regular pump silhouette would slip off the foot instantly during a stride.
-   **The Solution:** The pattern incorporates a wide, structural secure wrap strap made of vegetable-tanned **CIST Biopharma biological calfskin leather** that feeds directly through a reinforced channel cut into the rear crystal heel pillar. This strap anchors around the ankle bone, pulling her foot firmly backward down the true-ramp slope to hold the foot completely stationary inside the clear chassis.
