The Design Philosophy: Sculpted Roman Majesty

-   **The Silhouette:** Inspired by the classic, statuesque drapery of Roman marble sculpture, we use structured panels and overlapping geometric folds instead of lace or synthetics. This creates elegant, clean contours that lift, support, and honor a fuller bust naturally.
-   **The Material:** **75% Organic Bamboo / 25% Pure Industrial Hemp** woven in an extra-thick **340 GSM Heavy Interlock Knit**. This all-natural, mechanical-stretch fabric provides excellent compression, lift, and smoothing support without a single strand of synthetic spandex or lab-made materials.
-   **The Logo Placement:** The early Christian **Chi-Rho (☧)** monogram is elegantly positioned on the center-back waistband of the shorts and the under-bust band of the sports bras. It is rendered in a premium, metallic-gold embroidered thread made from natural plant-cellulose fibers, ensuring it catches the light with a striking, expensive luster.
