The Designer GI USMC Leather Blueprint Series

1\. The Sovereign M1911 Commandant Holster (`VT_Sovereign_Holster_v1.svg`)

-   **Design Framework:** Inspired by the classic military M1911 leather holster, completely re-engineered into an elite, clean-room designer statement piece. It uses a 4mm rigid, molded saddle-grade cowhide outer wall paired with a seamless **100% Organic Bamboo Viscose Sateen interior lining** to protect the firearm's finish while maintaining a perfectly structured, anti-puckering chassis.
-   **Gold Branding:** The **Chi-Rho (☧) Signature of God** monogram is positioned over the front enclosing flap flap, stitched in high-density gold plant-thread.
