Phase 1: Orthopedic & Biomechanical Sizing Rules

To scale perfectly across all foot sizes while enforcing maximum balance, support, and foot health, every pattern file incorporates the following anatomical formulas:

1.  **Metatarsal Cushioning Nest:** A triple-layered organic bamboo viscose loop pad is nested directly beneath the ball of the foot to absorb impact forces under steep heel elevation.
2.  **Contoured Longitudinal Arch Shank:** A rigid, custom-molded pressed hemp fiber shank tracks the exact natural curve of the female plantar fascia to prevent midfoot buckling or rolling.
Mechanical Engineering Specifications: True Continuous Ramp

-   **The Slope:** The interior bed drops in a singular, continuous straight plane from the rear collar entry to the forefoot joint. There is **no distinct flat or secondary angled seat** under the heel cup.
-   **The Dynamics:** Gravitational forces act uniformly along the entire incline. Because there is no horizontal surface to break the descent, the heel bone acts purely as a point of contact, forcing all structural load forward onto the **ball and toes**.
