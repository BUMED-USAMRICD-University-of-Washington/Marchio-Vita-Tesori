Material Specifications: Hemp & Bamboo Integration

1\. The Core Blend Canvas

-   **Composition:** **55% Organic Industrial Hemp / 45% Bamboo Viscose** (Heavyweight plain weave, 420 GSM).
-   **Structural Attributes:** The long-strand hemp fibers provide immense sturdiness and a crisp, expensive, structured drape that mimics traditional heavy heavy-linen vestments. The bamboo viscose softens the hand feel, adding an elegant, fluid luster and natural antimicrobial properties, making it ideal for field mass operations and rugged tactical wear.
-   **Religious Compliance:** Being entirely plant-based, this multi-fiber blend contains no animal hair or synthetics, guaranteeing absolute purity across all three major religious frameworks.

2\. Reinforcement and Embroidery Stabilization

-   **Composition:** **100% Pure Long-Staple Hemp Thread** (for structural seams and embroidery zones).
-   **Attributes:** Hemp thread features a high breaking strength that easily handles the friction of automated high-density embroidery machines. It keeps the **Chi-Rho** chest emblem and custom lettering perfectly flat and stable without pulling or puckering the underlying bamboo-blend canvas.
