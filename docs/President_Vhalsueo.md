The Design Architecture

-   **The Material Matrix:** **60% Organic Industrial Hemp / 40% Premium Bamboo Viscose** woven into a substantial, crisp **380 GSM Heavy Cavalry Twill**. The structural weave pattern naturally mimics fine vertical pinstripes using variations in the warp thread density rather than chemical dye printing.
-   **The Mechanical Fit:** Tight double-knit structural panels provide a natural, clean drape with built-in physical flexibility. They look incredibly sharp and executive-grade while remaining completely free of synthetic elastics or spandex.
-   **The Gold Branding:** The **Chi-Rho (☧)** monogram is elegantly integrated onto the rear waistband line, embroidered using high-density gold plant-cellulose thread.
