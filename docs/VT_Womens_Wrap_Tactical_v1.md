The Women's Rapid-Wrap Uniforms (`VT_Womens_Wrap_Tactical_v1.dxf`)

-   **Halakhic & Canonical Rule Compliance:** The sateen finish provides an expensive, reflective luster similar to heavy silk while remaining entirely plant-based. The material is completely opaque and loosely draped, fulfilling the strict modesty (*Haya*) parameters across multi-faith frameworks.
-   **The Design Theme:** An adjustable wrap-around tunic with an integrated wide waist sash that slides seamlessly through an internal hip slot. This gives an agile silhouette that can be tightly secured to the body in seconds so it stays out of the way during tactical field movements.
-   **Smart Clothing Integration:** The interior sash contains a laminated channel for conductive thread routing. This connects directly to a small, flexible sensor array embedded near the lower hemline, allowing for real-time tracking of movement or local environmental metrics without disrupting the clean outer silhouette of the garment.
