Material Sourcing & Technical Layering

1\. The Pure Plant Base Canvas

-   **Composition:** **65% Certified Organic Industrial Hemp / 35% Premium Bamboo Viscose** (Premium open-weave gauze, 280 GSM).
-   **Purity Matrix:** 100% free from any animal-derived proteins (zero silk) and completely free of lab-made synthetics or poly-elastanes. This ensures flawless compliance with traditional Jewish Halakhic Shatnez laws.

2\. The Actual Silver Thread Filament Track

-   **Thread Profile:** **99.9% Pure Sterling Silver Ribbon wrapped meticulously over a core of fine organic bamboo-microfiber strand.**
-   **Operational Performance:** This specialized hybrid yarn provides the classic weight, structural drape, and high-end luster of genuine precious metal while utilizing the plant-fiber core to withstand the intense tension and cyclic friction of automated industrial embroidery heads.
