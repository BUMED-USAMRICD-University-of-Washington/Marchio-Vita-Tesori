Mechanical Engineering Specifications for the Judge Holster

1\. Form-Molded Cylinder Clearance (Anti-Sag Grid)

-   **The Problem:** The unusually wide, elongated 5-shot cylinder of the Taurus Judge shifts the center of gravity outward, causing regular holsters to flop away from the body or stretch out of shape under active movement.
-   **The Solution:** This blueprint incorporates a multi-paneled, heavy-contoured front face. The leather is designed to be wet-molded directly around the exact dimensions of the Judge cylinder and barrel frame. A reinforced 5mm thick leather vertical backplane locks the holster flat against the 2-inch duty belt, preventing twisting or torque pull when the weapon is fully loaded.

2\. Open-Bottom Clearance Channel

-   **The Utility:** The lower muzzle terminal is cut wide and left entirely open. This prevents debris, spent casing fragments, or moisture from trapping inside the barrel channel during rugged field or facility duty, ensuring clean drawing clearances under all operational conditions.

3\. Thumb-Break Retention Flap with Brass Snap

-   **The Security:** Features a rigid, upward-extending leather thumb-break strap. A heavy-duty, solid cast brass mechanical snap fastener secures the hammer down. The reinforcement lines are stitched with double-needle lockstitching to ensure a reliable, crisp break when drawing, keeping the weapon perfectly locked during physical combat maneuvers or running.
