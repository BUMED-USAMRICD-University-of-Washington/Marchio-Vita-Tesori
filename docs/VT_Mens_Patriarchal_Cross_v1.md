Men's Imperial Patriarchal Pendant (`VT_Mens_Patriarchal_Cross_v1.scad`)

-   **Design Framework:** Modeled after the heavy gold cross pendants worn by Byzantine military-church governors. It utilizes a **Patriarchal (Double-Bar) architecture**, where the smaller upper bar represents the titulus (inscription plate) and the wide lower bar provides geometric balance.
-   **Biomechanical Integrity:** Designed as a solid, flat-backed, heavy relief piece that sits weighted and balanced flat against a canvas *Sovereign Duty Coat* or clinical *Chai Scrub* top, preventing uncomfortable swinging or flipping during rapid physical deployment.
