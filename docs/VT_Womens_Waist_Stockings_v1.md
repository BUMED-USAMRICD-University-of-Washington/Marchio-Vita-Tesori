The Waist Stockings / Tights with Strap Access (`VT_Womens_Waist_Stockings_v1.svg`)

-   **Design Framework:** High-waisted, full-length compression tights incorporating your new **strap access design option**.
-   **The Strap Access Mechanic:** The lower pelvis region features a reinforced cross-over slit panel held closed by low-profile, adjustable self-fabric bamboo straps. This allows the groin section to be parted and swung cleanly out to the flanks for seamless personal utility or medical assessment access without removing the high-compression waist harness.
-   **Branding Configuration:** The gold **Chi-Rho logo** is embroidered cleanly on the **middle of the back** compression belt panel.
