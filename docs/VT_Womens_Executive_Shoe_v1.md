Mechanical Engineering Specifications for the Pump

1\. Zero-Squeeze Forefoot Box Architecture

-   **The Cut:** Modified with a broad, anatomically graded square-oval toe box boundary.
-   **Performance:** Unlike standard fashion heels that taper sharply and pinch the phalanges, this pattern leaves a wide, relaxed room envelope. The **70% Bamboo / 30% Hemp Interlock Lining** allows the toes to splay naturally under load without friction or squeezing.

2\. Forward-Biased Slanted Shank (Anti-Heel Rest)

-   **The Geometry:** The internal support shank features a steep, uninterrupted downhill slope from the heel cup straight to the metatarsal pad zone.
-   **The Mechanism:** The top of the heel block is cut at an aggressive **15-degree forward pitch** rather than being flat. This structural slope makes it mechanically impossible for the calcaneus (heel bone) to rest or lock weight vertically over the heel post.

3\. Permanent Forward Slide & Pad Load

-   **The Dynamics:** Because the heel seat is slanted forward, any downward gravitational force immediately slides the foot's weight down the rigid ramp, transferring all body weight **ONLY onto the metatarsal pads and toes**. The heel post acts solely as a balancing kickstand; if the wearer attempts to lean back onto the heel, the angled top automatically drives the foot forward onto the forefoot pad.
