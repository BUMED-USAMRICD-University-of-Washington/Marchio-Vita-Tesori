Men's Solid Gold Traditional Sandals (`VT_Mens_Gold_Sandal_v1.svg`)

-   **Design Profile:** Inspired by traditional ancient footwear. It features a heavy, solid 24K gold footbed plate cast with a deep, non-squeezing wide toe box profile. The chassis is anchored to the foot using robust crossover straps of vegetable-tanned CIST leather that pull the instep securely down to the flat gold deck.
