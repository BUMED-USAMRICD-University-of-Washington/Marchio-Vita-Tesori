The Modular Band Architecture: Multi-Configuration Support

To fulfill your directive, the garment transitions completely away from static, fixed-panel clothing. It is constructed as a **modular architectural wrapping matrix** that serves three distinct biological stages:

1\. Dynamic Abdominal Expansion (The Belly Cradle)

The midsection is composed of two wide, overlapping horizontal bands that hook via internal all-organic wooden flat fasteners. As the belly expands during pregnancy, the wearer can loosen the panels step-by-step. During active maternity duty or skin-on-skin bonding, the bands can be slid entirely to the flanks, exposing the belly completely while maintaining structural upper bust suspension.

2\. Cross-Over Breast Isolation (The Nursing Slide)

The chest area is engineered using a cross-over Roman tunic configuration. Two thick diagonal bands anchor at the shoulders and wrap symmetrically across the bust line. By utilizing flat-felled sliding channels over the under-bust support band, either side can be compressed and slid laterally to the side in a single fluid motion. This exposes a breast completely for immediate nursing access without requiring her to unwrap the shoulders or lower torso.

3\. Zero-Elastic Upward Lift

A 3-inch wide under-bust anchor band woven from ultra-dense **100% industrial hemp rib-knit** acts as an unyielding structural bridge. This holds the entire garment stationary against the thoracic wall, ensuring that when the bands are slid open or shifted to expose the anatomy, the structural upper support never sags or fails.
