The Abdominal Cradle Camisole (`VT_Womens_Cradle_Top_v1.svg`)

-   **Design Framework:** A long-line duty camisole designed to sit flat under tactical uniform blouses.
-   **Anatomical Framing:** Features two mirroring, life-sized embroidered hand matrices positioned on the interior front lower panel. The palms wrap inward from the pelvic flanks to cup and cradle the lower abdomen naturally.
