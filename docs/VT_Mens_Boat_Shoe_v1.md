Mechanical & Design Architecture

-   **The Zero-Squeeze Forefoot:** Graded with an anatomically wide, expanded toe box boundary that lets the toes splay naturally, eliminating painful squeezing or pinching during active naval maneuvers.
-   **The 360-Degree CIST Leather Strap System:** True to the classic boat shoe layout, a continuous strap of raw, vegetable-tanned CIST Biopharma leather wraps entirely around the collar of the foot through reinforced eyelets. This structural integration allows the wearer to cinch the heel collar tight, locking the foot into the bed to ensure zero heel slippage without requiring synthetic polymers.
-   **The Wet-Deck Sole:** The vulcanized natural tree rubber outsole is scored with razor-thin, wavy siping lines. This structural tread patterns channels water out from under the foot, providing maximum slip resistance on wet, metal decks.
