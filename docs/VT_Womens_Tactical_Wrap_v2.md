Mechanical & Design Architecture

1\. Form-Fitting Core Compression (Sleek Taper)

-   **The Cut:** The midsection eliminates the deep gestational grading loops. Instead, the horizontal torso bands are cut with a sharp, anatomical compression taper that acts as a natural shapewear corset, smoothing the waistline using only the structural weight of the **340 GSM Bamboo/Hemp Swim Interlock**.

2\. Cross-Over Slide Tracks (Nursing/Exposure Versatility)

-   **The Mechanism:** The upper bust paneling features the exact cross-over diagonal band matrix. Operating over a rigid **3-inch industrial hemp rib-knit under-bust band**, either chest panel can slide fluidly to the side flank for instant exposure or skin-on-skin access, while the shoulders remain completely anchored.

3\. Low-Friction Quick-Slide Rear Open Seat

-   **The Utility:** The lower chassis retains the identical **Tri-Zone Sliding Suspension Matrix**. The crescent-cut rear seat panel passes through a flat-felled groin gusset tracking channel. The wearer can slide the rear paneling laterally to her hips to use the facilities instantly without removing upper garments, snapping it tautly back into center alignment when finished.
