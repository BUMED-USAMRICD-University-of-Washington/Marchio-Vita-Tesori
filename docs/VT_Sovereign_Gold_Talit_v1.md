Material Sourcing & Technical Layering

1\. The Pure Plant Base Canvas

-   **Composition:** **65% Certified Organic Industrial Hemp / 35% Premium Bamboo Viscose** (Premium open-weave gauze, 280 GSM).
-   **Structural Properties:** The hemp provides an authentic, ancient, textured weight that allows the garment to drape with absolute solemnity over the shoulders. The bamboo viscose softens the touch completely, giving it an expensive, breathable feel.
-   **Purity Matrix:** 100% free from any animal-derived proteins (zero silk) and completely free of lab-made synthetics or poly-elastanes. This ensures flawless compliance with traditional Jewish Halakhic Shatnez laws.

2\. The Actual Gold Thread Filament Track

-   **Thread Profile:** **99.9% Pure 24K Gold Ribbon wrapped meticulously over a core of fine organic bamboo-microfiber strand.**
-   **Operational Performance:** This specialized hybrid yarn provides the luxury and weight of genuine gold while utilizing the plant-fiber core to withstand the intense tension, pulling, and cyclic friction of automated industrial embroidery heads.
