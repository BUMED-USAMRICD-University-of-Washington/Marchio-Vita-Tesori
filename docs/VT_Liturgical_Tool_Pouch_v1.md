The Liturgical Facility & Church Tool Pouch (`VT_Liturgical_Tool_Pouch_v1.svg`)

-   **Design Framework:** A compartmentalized, hard-walled leather pouch engineered to keep tuning forks, anointing oil stocks, small relative calibration tools, and communion vessels upright and quiet. Woven inner partitions from **100% Organic Bamboo Sateen** insulate the items from clinking or creating metallic noise during movement.
