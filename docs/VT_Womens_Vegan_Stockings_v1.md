The Mechanical Engineering of Spandex-Free Hosiery

1\. The Pure Plant Micro-Mesh Matrix

-   **Composition:** **85% Mercerized Organic Bamboo Viscose / 15% Ultra-Fine Industrial Hemp Thread** (Knit into a high-gauge, breathable chevron micro-mesh).
-   **The Elasticity:** Bamboo viscose can be spun into incredibly fine, strong, micro-filaments. When processed on a high-density, multi-feed circular hosiery machine using an **interlocking loop configuration**, the knit yields a natural **40% mechanical stretch and recovery rate**. The addition of fine-strand hemp provides structural durability, preventing accidental runs or tears when worn beneath heavy *CIST Biopharma leather combat boots*.

2\. The Natural Tree Rubber Friction Grip (The No-Slip Band)

-   **The Problem:** Without synthetic spandex or elastic bands to constrict the leg, standard plant-fiber stockings would slide straight down to the ankles during physical deployment.
-   **The Solution:** The wide 3-inch upper thigh cuff is knitted from a dense **100% organic cotton rib-knit**. The interior wall of this cuff features parallel, screen-printed wavy ribbon tracks of **vulcanized liquid natural tree rubber (Hevea brasiliensis latex)**. This organic sap creates a high-friction, tacky grip directly against the skin, locking the stocking firmly to the thigh using surface friction rather than painful constriction.

3\. Anatomical Arch and Metatarsal Support

-   **The Comfort:** The foot segment incorporates a reinforced, double-knitted base panel under the metatarsal pad. This cushioning matrix absorbs impact forces and accommodates the continuous forward slide enforced by your **True-Ramp continuous slope high heels**, completely preventing friction blisters.
