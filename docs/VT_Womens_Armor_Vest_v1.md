Mechanical & Biomechanical Engineering

1\. The Back-Brace Support Lumbar Grid

-   **The Structure:** The rear paneling is cut as a single, wide, continuous vertical plate reinforced with flat-felled double rows of heavy-duty hemp stitching.
-   **The Function:** This mimics a medical or weightlifting back brace, enforcing a tall, rigid, statesque posture and providing deep lumbar support across the spine without restricting lung expansion or compressing the internal organs.

2\. The Under-Bust Elevation Shelf (Crescent Lift)

-   **The Curve:** The upper front boundary is cut into a deep, sweeping, rigid double-crescent architecture that traces the exact lower root line of the breasts.
-   **The Lifting Force:** Rather than squeezing the anatomy inward or hiding it behind flat chest plates, the 4mm hard-molded leather acts as a solid, unyielding structural shelf. It pushes upward from underneath, lifting and framing the breasts so they dynamically overflow the vest's top edge, remaining fully exposed while providing a secure, comfortable lift.

3\. Low-Friction Blouse Layer Compatibility

-   **The Dynamics:** When a linen or bamboo blouse is worn beneath the vest, the smooth bamboo-sateen interior lining eliminates fabric friction. The rigid front leather shelf maintains its upward push through the shirt material, allowing the fabric-wrapped bust to overhang the upper leather rim cleanly.
